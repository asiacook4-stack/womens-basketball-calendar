What to expect

Official schedules are checked about every six hours. Changes to an existing game require a second matching observation. Apple checks subscriptions on its own schedule, so updates are not immediate. On Mac, use View → Refresh Calendars when needed.

ESPN Unlimited and Peacock Premium coverage is labeled in event notes. B1G+, FOX/BTN and other services may need separate access. Game deep links are preferred; service directories are labeled. A reachable link does not guarantee playback or login access. Unannounced times and broadcasts stay TBA.

Unknown tournament opponents are excluded until confirmed. End times are estimates. “National spotlight” means matchups between the eight core teams and the additional contender pool documented in the repository; it is not every nationally televised game.
